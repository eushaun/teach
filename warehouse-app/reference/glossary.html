Domain — what planners talk about

Weeks of cover (WoC)
Weeks until projected stock first reaches zero, interpolated inside the week and capped at 52. Seasonal and arrival-aware: it is a reading of a projection, not stock ÷ rate. Defined in services/warehouse_velocity.py _cover_for (:416) using projection.Projection.weeks_of_cover (:188). A few figures still use plain division (the "plain-division register" in CLAUDE.md).
SOH (stock on hand)
Units physically at a location. History per day per location lives in daily_soh; the current snapshot in inventory_levels. models/daily_soh.py, models/inventory_level.py.
FTS (free to sell)
NetSuite quantityavailable (on hand minus committed). The opening stock of every cover projection. services/stock_cover.py get_physical_soh (:11).
Market total ("US Total")
The same cover reading over all of a market's stock against all-channel sales, with transfers excluded because they move stock inside the market. services/burndown.py get_batch_us_total_seasonal_woc (:594).
Hub / endpoint
A hub receives purchase orders and feeds the other warehouses (Riverside 124, Sydney LBO 4); its demand is the whole market's sales. Endpoints sell or fulfil. services/market_config.py hub_location_ids (:50).
Replenishment destination / route
An "irrecoverable" consignment location stock is shipped into (Amazon FBA 98 / 92, The Iconic 121). Its demand is the channels its route declares. market_config.ReplenishmentRoute; engine in services/fba_replenishment.py. L9
Transfer destination / route
A recallable move between the company's own warehouses (Riverside → Chicago, LBO ↔ LB). If the route names channels, the destination's demand is those channels times a demand share (Chicago: E-Com × 0.75). market_config.TransferRoute; engine in services/transfers.py. L10
Demand basis
What a location's demand is measured on: channel sales, or its own depletion. Chosen per location type, and shown on the page as a label such as "All-channel". services/demand.py demand_channels_for_location (:632), location_demand (:713). L3
Depletion (warehouse velocity)
Demand inferred from negative day-over-day SOH changes, excluding stockout days. The basis for any location with no declared channels (Walmart 127, Sydney LB 5). services/databricks_sync.py _rebuild_monthly_warehouse_velocity (:437).
Velocity (trailing)
Flat average units per week over lookback_weeks (default 6). Context only; the projection consumes the forecast rate. services/velocity.py get_batch_velocities (:9).
Forecast rate
The average weekly rate the projection actually consumed over the cover period. Stock, cover and this rate reconcile; stock ÷ trailing velocity generally does not. warehouse_velocity._cover_for (:416).
Seasonal forecast
Last year's three-month window around each future month (centre counted twice) times damped, capped growth, held within 0.25×–3× of the current level. demand.seasonal_forecast_detail (:178); constants at the top of demand.py. L3
Level
The current in-stock weekly rate over the last 8 complete weeks; it anchors and clamps the forecast. A measured level of zero means zero demand, except at an out-of-stock replenishment destination. demand.in_stock_level (:515).
Growth
Median year-on-year ratio over the last 4 months, damped halfway to 1 and capped at 0.5×–2×. demand.py (:283, GROWTH_* constants at the top).
Inbound grade
How much an incoming lot counts and when it lands: landed, in_transit, confirmed, planned or overdue, decided by dispatch evidence. services/inbound_grading.py grade_inbound (:242), _finalise (:113). L4
Overdue fade
An overdue lot counts 1 − late_days/60 of its quantity, reaching zero at 60 days late. Interim policy for POs that landed but were never receipted. inbound_grading._finalise; Matt's decision #4 in PROJECTION-ALIGNMENT-PLAN.md.
Transfer order (TO)
A NetSuite inter-location move. Open TOs arrive at ship date + route transit, and are dropped once past that date. transfer_orders table; inbound_grading.graded_incoming_by_location (:382).
PO / ex-factory date
A supplier purchase order and the date it leaves the factory (the revised date wins). Arrival = ex-factory + shipping transit (8 weeks, 4 for whirlygig). open_purchase_orders; sku_config.get_default_transit_weeks.
Transit weeks (route)
How long a move between two own locations takes: 3 weeks Riverside → Amazon, otherwise 2. Dates open TOs and when a recommended shipment lands. market_config.route_transit_weeks.
Lead time (order lead)
Factory order to landed stock, production included: 24 weeks standard, 4 for whirlygig. Used by Factory Buy only. sku_config.get_default_order_lead_weeks.
Case pack (box quantity)
Units per outer carton (NetSuite outer_quantity, default 24). Shipment quantities round up to whole cases unless the SKU is split-case eligible. services/case_packs.py; sku_master.box_quantity.
Whirlygig (WG)
The fast-moving core SKU family: short lead time and a different overstock threshold. The Dashboard defaults to showing WG only. sku_config.WHIRLYGIG_SKUS, duplicated by sku_master.is_whirlygig.
Polybag
An Amazon prep requirement; polybag lines go on a separate transfer file in the export. amazon_range.poly_bag_required.
Compliance hold (ship hold)
Blocks a SKU from Amazon shipments: Ship Qty forced to 0 and the SKU filtered from the export, while its need stays visible. services/compliance.py derive_hold, sync_compliance_to_range. L10
Range / range call
Which channels merch has ranged a SKU in (sku_market_status.channels), plus the Amazon range file in amazon_range. Gates Chicago transfers. databricks_sync.ranged_channels (origin/main; not in the local checkout).
Factory buy
The 12-month supplier order recommendation at market-total level. services/factory_buy.py get_factory_buy_recommendations (:79).
Safety stock
z × σ × √lead on the measured lead time. Computed in the replenishment engine but does not affect Ship Qty; it only feeds target stock. services/fba_replenishment.py (:622; :627 on origin/main).
Target stock
Safety stock plus promo buffer, displayed on the FBA/FBM Mix page. Not an input to the shipped quantity. fba_replenishment.py (:628; :633 on origin/main).
Fee floor (fee-risk threshold)
Weeks of cover (default 4) below which Amazon low-inventory fees apply. Demand rate × threshold is the floor that sizes Ship Qty. fba_replenishment_config.fee_risk_threshold_weeks; _run_burndown_need (:171).
FBA (Fulfilled by Amazon)
Stock held in Amazon fulfilment centres (NetSuite locations 98 / 92). amazon_fba_inventory.
FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant)
Amazon orders shipped from the company's own warehouse. The sync relabels them from the Amazon channel using a hardcoded US entity id; FBM counts as displaced FBA demand. databricks_sync.sync_daily_sales (:135).
AWD (Amazon Warehousing & Distribution)
Amazon's upstream overflow storage. Not sellable. amazon_awd_inventory.
AGL (Amazon Global Logistics)
Factory-direct shipments into Amazon; planners upload the tracker export. agl_shipments; routers/admin.py upload endpoint.
JAS ledger
The freight forwarder's inbound ledger with ETAs, synced as amazon_fba_inbound. A primary input to inbound grading. models/amazon_fba_inbound.py.
Stage / TOG
Product lifecycle stage, and the TOG thermal rating parsed from the product name ("1.0T"). sku_master.stage / tog.

Code — the engine's vocabulary

Projection (projection.project)
The one stock-projection primitive: steps an opening balance through contiguous periods, closing = opening + arrivals − demand, nothing floored. Every cover figure, order quantity and urgency band is a reading of one. services/projection.py project (:241), Projection (:168). L2
DemandSeries
A location × SKU's demand: monthly forecast rates, trailing rate, basis, scope and method. It supplies the projection's periods (weekly_periods). services/demand.py (:318).
Reading
A question asked of a finished projection: weeks_of_cover, min_point, deficit_against, gap_before (plus first_stockout). Policy lives in the inputs, never in a forked loop. projection.py (:179–238). L2
Arrivals (early_arrivals)
Dated, weighted quantities from inbound grading. An arrival lands in the period that contains its date; one dated before the first period is credited or dropped, and the caller must choose. projection.Arrival (:82), arrivals_from_dicts (:292).
CALC_BASIS_VERSION
A version string (YYYY-MM-DD.N) baked into every cache key. Bump it in the same commit as any projection, velocity or grading change, or users keep seeing old numbers. services/cache.py (:20).
Response cache
In-process dict of computed JSON responses. No TTL; cleared only by a successful sync, an admin mutation or a restart. Out-of-band DB edits stay invisible until then. services/cache.py make_key, invalidate.
Market param
?market= on every dashboard request, added by the axios interceptor and resolved by the get_market_param dependency. Every service defaults market="US", so a forgotten market=market silently breaks AU. middleware/auth.py (:181); frontend/src/api/client.ts (:24).
Sync step
One named stage of the daily Databricks → Postgres sync, logged as a sync_log row and shown in Settings. 18 on origin/main (17 locally), counting cluster_warmup. Most steps abort the whole run on failure. routers/sync.py SYNC_STEPS; services/databricks_sync.py. L5
Full replace vs incremental upsert
Full replace: DELETE then INSERT the whole table (POs, TOs, inventory levels, most Amazon tables). Incremental upsert: re-pull a trailing window and ON CONFLICT update (daily_sales 7 days, daily_soh 45 days). databricks_sync.run_incremental_sync (:1703).
Harness (baseline dump)
The projection regression gate: dump every engine's output before and after a change, then compare. The repo's only test suite, and it is run by hand. backend/app/tools/compare_projections.py. L8
CHANGELOG (app version)
The version of record is CHANGELOG[0].version in the sidebar component; there are no git tags. frontend/src/components/Layout.tsx (:8).

Infra — where it runs

Caddy
The reverse proxy on the EC2 box that terminates TLS (Let's Encrypt) and routes to the app container. It is shared with other apps on the same box. The repo copy of the Caddyfile is a stale reference. Caddyfile (reference only).
ECR
Amazon's container registry. The pipeline pushes each image tagged with its commit SHA and latest; old SHAs make rollback possible. bitbucket-pipelines.yml.
SSM (Systems Manager)
How the pipeline reaches the box: send-command runs a pull + docker compose up. It is also the only way to get a shell (no SSH). scripts/deploy.sh.
APScheduler
The in-process scheduler that fires the daily sync at 12:00 UTC inside the web process. This is why production pins uvicorn to one worker. backend/app/main.py (:37).
MSAL / Entra ID
Microsoft login. The SPA gets an Entra ID token via @azure/msal-browser and sends it as a Bearer token; the backend validates it against Entra's keys. A separate dev-only login mode also exists. frontend/src/auth/msalConfig.ts; middleware/auth.py.
