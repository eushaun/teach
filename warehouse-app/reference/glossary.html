Domain — markets, locations, stock

DreamCast
The app's name since v2.3 (13 Oct 2026). The repo, database, image and AWS names keep the old warehouse-forecast name. CLAUDE.md (:3-5); main.py FastAPI(title="DreamCast").
Market
One of US, AU, UK, EU, CA: its own warehouses, routes and settings. Every table holds every market, filtered by country, location or a market column. services/market_config.py MARKETS (:60-219).
Hub / endpoint
A hub receives purchase orders and feeds the other warehouses (Riverside 124, Sydney LBO 4, UK E-Com 65, EU E-Com 86, CA NAFG 110); its demand is all of the market's sales. Every other location is an endpoint; alerts and overstock iterate endpoints. MarketConfig.hub_location_ids, endpoint_location_ids; demand.demand_channels_for_location (:1059).
PO-receiving location
Where factory POs land (US: Riverside, Amazon and Chicago; elsewhere the hub). Feeds market-total arrivals and the factory buy split. MarketConfig.po_receiving_locations.
Transfer route / destination
A recallable move between the company's own warehouses (Riverside → Chicago, LBO ↔ LB, UK/EU E-Com → NuOrder). If the route names channels, the destination's demand is those channels times a demand share (Chicago: E-Com × 0.75). market_config.TransferRoute (:15-33); engine services/transfers.py. L11
Replenishment route / destination
A move into an irrecoverable consignment location: Amazon FBA (98, 92, 66, 87, 125) or The Iconic (121). Its demand is the channels the route declares (Amazon + FBM; Marketplace for The Iconic). market_config.ReplenishmentRoute (:35-41); engine services/fba_replenishment.py. L10
Route transit
Ship-to-receipt weeks for a route: 3 for Riverside → Amazon, otherwise 2 (overridable per market for replenishment). Dates open transfer orders and when an order lands. market_config.route_transit_weeks (:228).
SOH (stock on hand)
Units physically at a location. Daily history in daily_soh; the current snapshot in inventory_levels. models/daily_soh.py, models/inventory_level.py.
FTS (free to sell)
NetSuite quantityavailable (on hand minus committed). The opening stock of every cover projection. services/stock_cover.py get_physical_soh (:11).
Weeks of cover (WoC)
Weeks until projected stock first reaches zero, interpolated inside the week and capped at 52. Seasonal and arrival-aware: a reading of a projection, not stock ÷ rate. warehouse_velocity._cover_for (:416); projection.Projection.weeks_of_cover (:188).
Market total ("US Total")
The same reading over all of a market's stock against all-channel sales, with POs at PO-receiving locations and transfers excluded (they move stock inside the market). The JSON field is still us_total in every market. burndown.get_batch_us_total_seasonal_woc (:602).

Domain — demand

Demand basis
What a location's demand is measured on: channel sales, or its own depletion. Picked per location type (hub: all channels; replenishment or channel transfer destination: the route's channels; anything else: depletion), and shown as a label such as "Amazon + FBM sales". demand.demand_channels_for_location (:1059), location_demand (:1140), Basis (:150). L3
Depletion
Demand inferred from negative day-over-day SOH changes, stockout days excluded. The basis for locations with no declared channels (Walmart 127, Sydney LB 5). databricks_sync._rebuild_monthly_warehouse_velocity (:472).
Velocity (trailing) vs forecast rate
Velocity is a flat average over lookback_weeks (default 6), shown for context. The forecast rate is the average the projection actually consumed over the cover period; stock, cover and forecast rate reconcile, stock ÷ velocity generally doesn't. velocity.get_batch_velocities (:25); warehouse_velocity.py (:398-406).
Seasonal forecast
Last year's three-month window around each future month times damped growth (0.5×–2×, from the last 4 months), held within 0.25×–3× of the current level. Launch-year SKUs are forecast flat at their level. demand.seasonal_forecast_detail (:264); constants (:40-65). L3
Level / level source
The current weekly rate that anchors the forecast, and how it was reached: in_stock, earlier_rate (out of stock: keep the old rate), few_sales, intermittent, sibling. demand.in_stock_level (:752); sources (:139-143). L4
One-off orders
Weekly volume above 3× the 13-week normal is split into a separate stream forecast at its long-run rate, so a single big order doesn't inflate base demand. All-channel series only. demand.split_one_offs (:625). L4
Few sales
A SKU first sold under 13 weeks ago with fewer than 3 selling weeks: everything sold is spread over 13 weeks (v2.26). demand.few_sales_level (:769).
Intermittent
On a scope without FBA, a SKU selling in under half its weeks takes the plain 13-week average (v2.26). demand.intermittent_level (:785).
Sibling launch rate
A new line at a channel transfer destination takes the median rate of the same product and size in other colours (v2.26). Drives first transfers of new lines. demand.sibling_launch_rates (:988).
Planned adjustment
A planner's extra units over a date range, or a rate multiplier, per SKU and market; attached to the all-channel series (factory buy, market totals). Part of the record. services/demand_adjustments.py; table demand_adjustments (migration 056).
Promo calendar
Per-market uplift entries that scale the weekly rates they overlap, on top of last year's seasonality. Since v2.22.1 they change Ship Qty (not the fee floor or a launch quantity). fba_replenishment._apply_promos (:123); table fba_promo_calendar.

Domain — inbound stock

Inbound grade
landed, in_transit, confirmed, planned or overdue per lot, decided from shipping milestones and dates. A cover grade is one that counts as cover. inbound_grading.py (:82-101), grade_inbound (:418). L5
Graded arrival
The one arrival date every engine uses for a lot (never before today), with a weight (share counted) and late_days. inbound_grading._finalise (:153); graded_incoming_by_location (:528), graded_incoming_market_total (:638).
Overdue fade
An overdue, undispatched lot counts 1 − late_days/60 of its quantity; a lot landed more than 14 days ago but never receipted fades too. Transfer orders past their date fade the same way (since v2.10) rather than vanishing. inbound_grading.py (:52-59), _finalise.
Dispatch evidence
Amazon destinations (98, 92, 66, 87, 125) need proof a lot has shipped, so a planned lot doesn't count there. inbound_grading.py (:450); market_config.AMAZON_DEST_LOCATIONS (:326).
Shipping master
dwh.shipping.master_ns_jas_inbound_shipment_pos: one row per shipment × PO line, JAS and AGL, every market. Grading's first source since v2.18; open POs count only where the master doesn't describe them. Owned outside ltd-dbx-asset-bundle. models/inbound_shipment_po.py; inbound_grading._master_lots (:335); sync step inbound_shipments.
JAS
The freight forwarder for factory → warehouse shipments. Its rows in the shipping master carry NetSuite open units. (The old JAS ledger table, amazon_fba_inbound, is retired.) shipping master.
AGL (Amazon Global Logistics)
Factory-direct shipments into Amazon. Bookings come from Databricks into agl_booking (the old upload flow was removed in v2.18). agl_booking; inbound_grading._agl_bookings_received (:314).
AWD (Amazon Warehousing & Distribution)
Amazon's upstream storage. Not sellable; display only. amazon_awd_inventory.
FBA / FBM
Fulfilled by Amazon (stock in Amazon fulfilment centres) / by Merchant (Amazon orders shipped from the company's own hub). The sync relabels each market's FBM customer as channel FBM; FBM counts as displaced FBA demand. daily_sales.channel; databricks_sync.sync_daily_sales (:157); market_config.fbm_entity_ids().
Transfer order (TO)
A NetSuite inter-location move. Open TOs arrive at ship date + route transit and fade once overdue. The weekly Amazon order becomes TOs raised in NetSuite, which the next sync brings back. transfer_orders; inbound_grading.graded_incoming_by_location (:528).
PO / ex-factory date
A supplier purchase order and the date it leaves the factory. Without shipping data, arrival = ex-factory + shipping transit (8 weeks standard, 4 whirlygig). open_purchase_orders; sku_config.get_default_transit_weeks (:83).

Domain — quantities and orders

Fee floor (fee-risk threshold)
demand rate × fee_risk_threshold_weeks (default 4): the stock level below which Amazon low-inventory fees apply, and the floor Ship Qty is sized to. fba_replenishment_config; fba_replenishment._run_burndown_need (:186).
Ship Qty / need / unavoidable gap
Need = the deficit against the fee floor from the week the order can land. Capped and rounded to cases, it becomes Ship Qty. A dip before the order can land is reported as the unavoidable gap, not ordered. fba_replenishment._run_burndown_need (:186). L10
Source cap (wanted / cut_by_reserve / cut_by_stock)
A shipment can't exceed what the source can spare. Since v2.26 the order shows what the cap cost: the need in whole cases, what the e-com reserve held back, what the source lacked. fba_replenishment.py; case_packs.resolve (:50).
E-com reserve
Stock held at the source for e-commerce: NetSuite safetystocklevel plus manual stock_reservations. stock_cover.get_ecom_reserves (:76).
Safety stock / target stock
z × σ × √lead on the measured lead time. Computed and returned as part of target_stock, but it does not affect Ship Qty and nothing displays it. fba_replenishment.py (:772-783).
Launch (New to Amazon)
A ranged SKU with under 4 weeks of route sales. A ticked launch replaces the need with 4 weeks × a starting rate, capped at 75% of the source. services/amazon_launch.py.
Case pack (box quantity) / split case
Units per outer carton (NetSuite outer_quantity, default 24; an unconfirmed one shows an amber ?). Quantities round to whole cases unless a SKU is flagged split-case eligible (off for every SKU since v2.25 unless set). services/case_packs.py; sku_master.box_quantity, split_case_eligible.
Case pack override ("Amazon pack")
A per-SKU, per-market box size used for Amazon only; such lines always go on the split-case export file. sku_range.case_pack_override; case_packs.overrides_for (:140).
Whirlygig (WG)
The fast-moving core family: monthly factory buys, 4-week shipping, 10-week targets, its own overstock threshold. The Dashboard shows WG only by default. The flag is sku_master.is_whirlygig. services/sku_config.py (:1-30, :79).
Factory buy
The supplier order recommendation: one market-total projection stepped over retail weeks; each order carries stock to the next landing and closes at target cover (10 weeks WG, 20 standard) × that week's rate. Lost sales before the first landing aren't reordered; passed months fold forward. factory_buy.get_factory_buy_recommendations (:311). L11
Retail calendar
LTD's Saturday–Friday weeks in 4-4-5 months, with the financial year starting the Saturday on or before 1 July. Used by the factory buy. services/retail_calendar.py (:87).
Buy cadence / order deadline
WG buys monthly (deadline end of retail week 1, ex-factory next month); standard only in retail Jan / Apr / Jul / Oct (deadline end of week 3, ex-factory five months on); then shipping 4 / 8 weeks. sku_config.py (:83-105).
Destination split
How a US factory order is divided between Chicago, Amazon and Riverside. Other markets: hub only. The harness snapshots the total, not the split. factory_buy.py (_allocate, months[].split).

Domain — the SKU record

SKU status
Lifecycle label per SKU and market (ONGOING, NEW, RUNDOWN, WRITE OFF…), owned in DreamCast since v2.24. Its config says whether it replenishes; a no-replenish status hides a SKU everywhere. status_manual marks one set by a person (amber dot). sku_range.status; sku_status.get_batch_sku_statuses (:59); sku_status_config. L12
Market status
Merch's second per-market label: the collection or launch ("NEW … SEPT 26"). Free text; no calculation reads it. sku_range.market_status.
Range / range call / range gate
Whether a SKU is sold through a channel in a market: yes / no / undecided per ecom, amazon_fba, amazon_fbm, wholesale, marketplace. Owned in DreamCast since v2.22; the merch file only fills undecided calls. Replenishment, transfers and the factory buy split drop SKUs decided not-ranged for the route; undecided never gates. services/sku_range.py ranged_for (:104), get_range_skus (:59), seed_from_file (:262). L12
Range exceptions / range gap
Exceptions: ranged-not-selling, ticked-no-but-selling, selling-not-ranged. A range gap is a SKU selling through a destination's channels but never held there. range_exceptions.build_exceptions (:187); transfers.py (range_gap).
Seller SKU
The Amazon Merchant SKU a NetSuite SKU ships under: a planner's override, else amazon_sku_mapping, else the NetSuite SKU. Used in the Send to Amazon manifest. sku_range.seller_sku_lookup (:316).
Polybag
An Amazon prep requirement; polybag lines go on their own export file. A planner's choice (poly_bag_manual) is kept. sku_range.poly_bag_required; seed.seed_polybag_flags.
Compliance hold
Our shipping block for an Amazon ASIN, derived from Amazon's compliance register during the sync: a live listing is never held; a down listing only for a reason Amazon has open today. A hold is superseded when the SKU sells on another, unheld ASIN. A held line ships 0 with its need visible. Our compliance case is annotation; only manual_hold blocks. compliance.derive_hold (:78), superseded_holds (:148), sync_compliance_to_range (:196).
Phantom stock
Units NetSuite shows at an Amazon location that Amazon doesn't report (NetSuite receipts TOs on ship). The Amazon Stock Gap page reconciles it. services/amazon_stock_gap.py; forecast-calculations.md (:653-661).
Stage / TOG
Product lifecycle stage, and the TOG thermal rating parsed from the product name ("1.0T"). sku_master.stage / tog.

Code — the engine's vocabulary

Projection (projection.project)
The one stock-projection primitive: steps an opening balance through contiguous periods, closing = opening + arrivals − demand, nothing floored. Every cover figure, order quantity and urgency band is a reading of one. Unchanged since 1 Oct 2026. services/projection.py project (:241), Projection (:168). L2
DemandSeries
A location × SKU's demand: monthly forecast rates, trailing rate, basis, scope, method, level source and adjustments. rate_for(d) is demand in the week starting d; it supplies the projection's periods (weekly_periods). services/demand.py (:412-514). L3
Reading
A question asked of a finished projection: weeks_of_cover, min_point, deficit_against, gap_before (plus first_stockout). projection.py (:168-238). L2
Arrivals (early_arrivals)
Dated, weighted quantities from inbound grading. An arrival lands in the period that contains its date; one dated before the first period is credited (cover engines) or dropped (burndown charts), and the caller must choose. projection.Arrival; project (:241-256).
Policy
Rules applied to a reading, never inside the loop: source caps and e-com reserves, case packs, range gates, compliance holds, launch overrides. case_packs.py, sku_range.py, compliance.py, amazon_launch.py.
Review rules / auto-approval
Rules that flag a line "to look at more closely" with a reason (few sales, large quantity, above recent rate…) and never change a quantity. On the weekly order, in-band unadjusted lines are auto-approved. replenishment_rules.py (review_reasons :120), transfer_rules.py, factory_buy_rules.py.
Order stage
The weekly replenishment order (one per market, route and ISO week) moves draft → ready (submitted) → approved. Every write is audited in replenishment_order_event. The status column is something else (draft / approved / exported). replenishment_orders.stage_of (:51); services/order_events.py.
The record
Tables DreamCast is the source of truth for, written by planners and admins: sku_range, orders and their audit trail, demand adjustments, promos, compliance cases, reservations, launches. Rule: the file seeds, a decision wins. backend/app/models/CLAUDE.md.
Mirror / rollup
A mirror is a Postgres copy of a dwh table, written only by the sync. A rollup is a table the sync rebuilds from mirrors (monthly_velocity, monthly_warehouse_velocity). services/databricks_sync.py.
CALC_BASIS_VERSION
A version string (YYYY-MM-DD.N) that prefixes every cache key. Bump it in the same commit as any projection, velocity or grading change so old entries can't be served. services/cache.py (:20).
Response cache
An in-process dict of computed JSON responses. No TTL; cleared at the end of a successful sync, by relevant admin changes, or by a restart. SQL fixes and out-of-process syncs never reach it. services/cache.py make_key, invalidate (:39-50).
Cache warm-up
The last sync step: recompute every cached page for every market at default parameters, so planners hit a warm cache. services/cache_warmup.py warm_cache (:60).
Market param
?market=, added by the axios interceptor and resolved by the get_market_param dependency against the user's markets. Every service defaults market="US", so a forgotten market=market silently computes US. middleware/auth.py (:216-229); frontend/src/api/client.ts (:32-39).
Sync step
One named stage of the Databricks → Postgres sync, logged as a sync_log row and shown in Settings: cluster_warmup, 16 data steps, cache_warmup. Steps 6–8 log and continue; the rest abort the run before the cache is cleared. routers/sync.py SYNC_STEPS (:30); databricks_sync.run_incremental_sync (:2150). L6
Full replace vs incremental upsert
Full replace: delete and reload the whole table (POs, TOs, inventory levels, most Amazon and inbound tables). Incremental upsert: re-pull a trailing window and ON CONFLICT update (daily_sales 7 days, daily_soh 45 days). databricks_sync.py.
Harness
The projection regression gate: dump every engine's output for every warehouse and SKU in every market before and after a change, then compare. Run by hand; refuses dumps from different days or across a sync. backend/app/tools/compare_projections.py; skill projection-harness. L9
Plain-division register
The list of figures still computed as stock ÷ trailing rate rather than as a projection reading (per-channel columns, Stock Review, style cover…). forecast-calculations.md (:683-727).
CHANGELOG (app version)
The version of record is CHANGELOG[0].version in the sidebar component (user-facing, plain language). Developer history is the root CHANGELOG.md. No git tags. frontend/src/components/Layout.tsx; CHANGELOG.md.
MCP server
A second front door in the same process, at /mcp, mounted only when MCP_ENABLED is true. Six read tools and two write tools; writes are preview then confirm with a change_token, and are audited with source mcp. backend/app/mcp/; MCP-PLAN.md.

Access and infra — who, and where it runs

Role
admin, planner, viewer, reader. A reader (the default for anyone at LTD on first sign-in, on every market) may GET only the endpoints in READER_ALLOWED_PATHS, enforced in the backend for every router. middleware/auth.py (:19-45); routers/admin.py VALID_ROLES (:70). L12
Approver
users.is_approver: may tick lines, approve and unlock the weekly order. Independent of role. services/order_permissions.py can_approve_order.
MSAL / Entra ID
Microsoft sign-in. The SPA gets an Entra ID token via @azure/msal-browser and sends it as a Bearer token; the backend validates it against Entra's published keys. Local and branch stacks can run with Entra blanked and a dev token instead. frontend/src/api/client.ts (:41-64); middleware/auth.py.
APScheduler
The in-process scheduler that fires the sync at 08:00 and 20:00 UTC inside the web process (dev stacks too). backend/app/main.py (:40-41).
One worker
Production runs uvicorn with --workers 1: the scheduler, response cache and rate limiters live in process memory, so a second worker would double-fire the sync and split the cache. Dockerfile (:36).
Caddy
The reverse proxy on the EC2 host that terminates TLS (Let's Encrypt) and routes to the app container; shared with two other apps on the host. The repo's Caddyfile is a stale copy. Caddyfile (reference only); skill prod-ops.
ECR
Amazon's container registry. The pipeline pushes each image tagged with its commit SHA and latest; old SHAs make rollback possible. bitbucket-pipelines.yml.
SSM (Systems Manager)
How the pipeline reaches the host (send-command: pull + docker compose up -d), and the way to get a shell there (no SSH). scripts/deploy.sh; skill prod-ops.
Branch test stack
Testing a branch against a copy of the local database (branch_test) with the worktree's backend mounted into a docker run of the dev image, so parallel sessions don't share state. skill test-branch-stack. L9
